FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you provide emergency elevator repair?

Yes. Elevator Hub offers emergency support for urgent elevator breakdowns and related faults. For immediate assistance, call 0312 5189356 or WhatsApp 0336 5642428.

Which areas do you serve?

Our service focus is Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Contact us with your location so we can confirm service availability.

How often should an elevator be maintained?

Maintenance frequency depends on elevator type, usage, manufacturer requirements and local safety requirements. A qualified technician should assess the appropriate maintenance schedule.

Can you inspect an elevator before giving repair advice?

Yes. An inspection can help identify the cause of a fault and determine what service or repair is appropriate.

Do you provide elevator installation?

Elevator Hub provides installation support. Contact the team to discuss the requirements of your project.

Do you service commercial and hospital elevators?

Yes. Commercial and hospital lift service is included in the business service offering.
